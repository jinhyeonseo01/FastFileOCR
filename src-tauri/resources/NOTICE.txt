Glyph OCR uses PaddleOCR-VL-1.6 and PP-DocLayoutV3 by PaddlePaddle (Apache-2.0), llama.cpp (MIT), PDFium (BSD-style licenses), and ONNX Runtime (MIT).
Full licenses and third-party notices are in licenses/.
Model weights are not bundled. The application downloads pinned, SHA-256 verified files from the official Hugging Face repositories.
The layout graph is generated from the Transformers Apache-2.0 implementation and references the unchanged official Safetensors weights externally.
chat-template.jinja adapts PaddlePaddle's Apache-2.0 template to accept image_url content.
